EASIEST OPTION
Rename your files to match the names above and drop them in. Done — nothing
else to change.

THE SONG
Save the audio file as `my-goal.mp3`. A Spotify or YouTube *link* can't be
played inside a page like this (they block it), so it has to be an actual
audio file sitting in this folder. It starts on its own when she taps
"Open my heart", fades up slowly over about 6 seconds, and stays quiet
underneath the words. If your file is a .m4a, keep the extension and change
`musicFile` in config.js instead of renaming it.

THE SURPRISE VIDEO
`us.mp4` is the one that appears at the very end, after she picks "Yes".
Keep it short and cute. It plays with sound, and the song automatically
ducks down while it's playing, then comes back after.

WANT DIFFERENT NAMES / MORE FILES?
Open config.js, find section 7 (THE GALLERY), and edit the `src` names.
Add as many lines as you want by copying an existing one. The surprise
video lives in section 11 (`finale.surprise.video`).

PORTRAIT vs LANDSCAPE
Mix them freely. Tall photos and Instagram-reel-shaped videos stay tall,
wide ones stay wide. Nothing gets squashed or badly cropped — the site
measures every file and gives it the right space. You don't have to do
anything.

FILE TIPS
• Photos: .jpg / .png / .webp — resize to about 2000px on the long edge so
  the site stays fast. Anything straight off a phone works too.
• Videos: .mp4 (H.264) plays everywhere. Keep them under ~30 MB each if you
  can, especially if she'll open this on mobile data.
• Song: .mp3 or .m4a. Don't rename a .m4a to .mp3 — update config.js instead.
